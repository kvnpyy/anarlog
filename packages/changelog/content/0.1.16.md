## Pro in Settings

- **Pro** is now a Settings item, under Profile — share Acorn for a year of Pro, or redeem an invite, without hunting through General
- The plans window from Teams or Developers no longer overflows; it scrolls, and **Share to get Pro** is the real action while checkout is closed
- **Open in Settings** from that window takes you to the Pro page
