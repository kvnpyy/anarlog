The update button installs the app, and a group call can tell the other voices apart.

## Updates

- The download notice stays still instead of flashing
- **Restart** stays on the notice when the download is finished, and clicking it is what installs the update

## Speakers

- People on the other end of a call can be separate speakers, even when the invite only lists one of them
- That split runs on recordings up to 30 minutes
