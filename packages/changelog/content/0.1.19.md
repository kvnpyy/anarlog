Lines you can actually say, a way out of the open note, and files in chat.

## Sound smart

- Talking points stay on your side of the call
- If you say you're the vendor, the next lines are yours

## Ask across meetings

- On a note, tap **Ask across meetings** to search your other meetings without closing the note
- Tap **Ask this meeting** to come back to that note's chat

## Attachments

- Paste, drop, or attach images, PDFs, and text files in chat
- Acorn sends them with your question
