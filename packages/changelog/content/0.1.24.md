Briefs only use meetings that belong to this one, and there is a second way to earn Pro.

## Pre-meeting briefs

- A brief uses earlier meetings with the same outside person, the same company, or the same recurring series
- One coworker in common is no longer enough to pull in an unrelated meeting
- Internal meetings still get a brief from the same title or the exact same people, including one-to-ones
- Each point names the meeting it came from
- When nothing earlier is about this meeting, the memo stays empty instead of filling with unrelated notes

## Pro

- Follow Acorn on LinkedIn from Settings → Pro or the Plans window to get 1 month of Pro
- That month adds on top of Pro time you already have, so sharing and following together give up to 4 months
- The 3 months for sharing are granted once instead of restarting each time the share card loads
