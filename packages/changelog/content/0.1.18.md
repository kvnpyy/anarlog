Names on the transcript, hour-long calls that finish, and a shorter Pro invite.

## Who's talking

- Your microphone is labeled as you
- If Acorn has heard someone on the invite before, it matches that voice and puts their name on the transcript
- If they say **I'm Ada** or **this is Ada speaking**, that cluster gets Ada
- When it's only pretty sure, the label is **Ada?** — tap **Confirm** once
- It only ever picks from people on the invite, not random names from the internet

## Long calls

- Hosted transcription now splits recordings around 10 minutes, then stitches them back together
- A ~60 minute meeting should finish instead of hanging or coming back empty

## Share for Pro

- Sharing Acorn grants **3 months** of Pro, not a year — in Settings, onboarding, and the actual grant
- People who already unlocked a longer expiry keep it

## Smart folders

- Internal meetings and customer meetings no longer land in the same pile because a teammate sat in both
- Folder names come from company, distinctive titles, or what you discussed
