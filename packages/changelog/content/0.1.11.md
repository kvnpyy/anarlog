Ask emails stay short, and you can take them into Gmail or Claude.

## Emails

- Drafted emails stay under 250 words unless you ask for a longer recap
- **Open in Gmail** puts the subject and body into a Gmail compose window

## Claude

- In Settings → Developers, **Add to Claude** lets Claude Desktop read your Acorn notes and summaries
- Restart Claude after adding, then ask it about a deal or meeting
