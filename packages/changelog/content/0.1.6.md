Acorn is getting out of the way so your notes can stay in front.

## Ask, right where you write

- Ask from the bottom of a note — one quiet composer, no extra star button covering your words
- Settings, calendar, and the rest of the app stay clean. Ask stays with your notes
- Follow-up emails come back short and human. Under 150 words, in your voice, ready to send

## Folders that keep up

- Recurring meetings file themselves into the right folder
- Acorn suggests folders from the people and titles you already use
- Ask can stay inside a folder when you want a tighter search

## Calendar you can trust

- Google Calendar reconnects more reliably after an update
- If Calendar went quiet, connect it once more — Acorn will remember

## Everyday polish

- Smaller fixes and quieter details throughout, so the app feels more like a notepad and less like a dashboard
