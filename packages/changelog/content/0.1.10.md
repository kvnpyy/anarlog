Ask stays with the note you're looking at, even if another meeting is still recording.

## The meeting you have open

- Open a previous meeting during a live call and Ask uses that note, not the current call
- Stay on the live meeting and Live Ask still uses what's being said right now

## Questions you can ask next

- When you ask for questions to ask, you get questions you can say — not questions for the other person
