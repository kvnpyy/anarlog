Acorn keeps the notepad in front, and Ask out of the way.

## Ask, then your notes again

- Ask lives under the note instead of covering the last lines
- Click the notes above Ask to shrink the thread back to the quiet bar. Click Ask to open it again
- Settings and the rest of the app stay clean. Ask stays with your notes

## Calendar that remembers

- Google Calendar reconnects more reliably after an update
- If Calendar went quiet, connect it once more — Acorn will remember

## Everyday polish

- What’s new now shows the notes for the version you actually have
- Smaller fixes so the app stays on its feet when a window goes blank or Calendar syncs
