Ask follows the note on screen, and live recaps stick to who actually talked.

## Ask on a note

- Switching meetings closes the previous note's Ask thread instead of carrying prompts across
- Draft email, Action items, and Key decisions sit centered above the chat box
- Meeting shortcut chips no longer appear under those prompts on a note you already have open

## Catch me up

- Recaps only treat labeled **You** lines as your speech
- If your microphone has been silent, it recaps the other speakers instead of writing as if you led
- Catch me up uses the last five minutes of the live transcript; Sound smart uses the last ten
